## Week 1–2: Data Collection, Understanding & Preprocessing

Week 1: Data Collection and Understanding
- Defined the project scope and objectives for smart energy consumption analysis.
- Collected the Smart Home Energy Consumption dataset.
- Explored the dataset structure, columns, and data types.
- Verified data integrity and checked for missing values.
- Performed initial exploratory data analysis to understand energy usage patterns.

Week 2: Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Converted date and time columns into a unified timestamp format.
- Handled timestamp inconsistencies and ensured proper datetime parsing.
- Removed abnormal values and outliers from energy consumption data.
- Resampled energy consumption data into hourly and daily formats.
- Visualized appliance-wise energy consumption trends.
- Saved the cleaned and processed dataset for use in feature engineering and modeling.
